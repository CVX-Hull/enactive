# Different decision horizons, fewer synchronous model calls

Research cutoff: **2026-10-24**. The question is how to make Enactive responsive
without sacrificing organic discovery, current feedback or independent learning.
The [Fresh024 review](acceptance/2026-10-24-fresh024-execution-review.md) supplies
the local evidence. This synthesis supports a proposed next slice, not a deployed
hierarchy, replicated research result or passing responsiveness claim.

Current-first searches covered 2026/2025 arXiv, ACL/conference surfaces and references,
including October submissions and contrary memory evidence before following older
foundations. Two independent scholarly lenses assessed control/skills and memory;
source and trace reviewers checked transfer assumptions. The selected primary
HTML/PDF methods, evaluations and limitations below were read; search snippets and
metadata-only leads do not support conclusions. This is a scoped search sample,
not an exhaustive SOTA survey. All selected 2026 arXiv entries remain preprints.

More machinery needs incremental benefit. |

## Synthesis: a hierarchy of time scales, not mandatory model tiers

Our engineering inference is to separate four responsibilities: atomic feedback
and accounting; short observation-conditioned procedures; current subgoal/mission
selection; and retrospective abstraction. A strategic horizon may be useful
without having a separate always-running strategic agent. The same background
actor can alternate compilation, diagnosis and upcoming-plan questions under its
existing finite budget; ordinary retrieval and bookkeeping require no LLM.

The convolutional-network analogy helps only with reusable local computation and
coarse-to-fine context. These agents do not share trained feature hierarchies,
translation equivariance or a demonstrated sufficient latent state. More model
levels add queueing, stale-state and correlated-error costs. Multi-rate feedback
and temporally extended options are closer design analogies, not convergence or
optimality guarantees. No new training, model provider or neural network follows.

Fresh024 also has multi-second adjacent ACK intervals inside local programs,
where no model runs. Local capture/evidence/accounting/feedback cost therefore
needs attention alongside model-task allocation; the retained evidence does not
isolate its cause. Architecture cannot hide that cost by adding agents.

For latency, useful work already executing can cover planning delay only while
that work remains justified. Turn-based waiting and ongoing engine activities
need separate evaluation; filler inputs cannot hide a stalled decision. If all
useful routines yield, the player must interpret the surprise or deliberately
wait for a focused answer. Another slow model is helpful only if the whole system
still meets the frozen response/quality criteria.

For memory, store source-linked episodes and revision/counterexample lineage;
derive exact exposure, selection, delivery, supported effects and costs in software.
Ambiguous success and applicability still need interpretation, possibly later.
Do not request per-key narration, infer attention from retrieval, or turn a usage
score into causal credit or a calibrated belief. Consolidation is selective and
optional; preserving an abstraction's text is not itself learning.

Select the [execution-first learning slice](design/asynchronous-reflection.md#execution-first-learning).
Functional acceptance must show an organically acquired routine, a real changed
condition, revision/abstention and independent later application while optional
learning cannot stall justified play. Separately measure useful-decision latency,
model calls, acquisition/repair, context, all actors and failures. Persistent
routines, raw episodes, temporary guidance and no-extra-learner conditions belong
to a matched-resource efficacy study if needed; a successful functional sequence
alone establishes neither causal benefit nor eventual convergence.

Lead maintains this synthesis at the stated change triggers and #26's existing
research-review checkpoint. New paper volume alone neither resets maintenance
dates nor justifies changing architecture. Revisit a selected decision when new
primary evidence or actual failures alter its transfer assumptions.
