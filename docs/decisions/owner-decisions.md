# Owner decision log

The owner's decisions in force, grouped by subject and newest first within each group.
This is the one list
([one home per fact](../documentation.md#one-authoritative-home-per-kind-of-fact)):
current pages state the resulting rule and link its row here, and do not retell the
decision. Decisions that were replaced, and approvals and rulings that are used, are in
the [archive](owner-decisions-archive.md).

- **A row is added in the change that acts on the decision**, or in a change of its own
  when the decision alters nothing else. It goes under the subject it belongs to and
  starts with an anchor whose id says what it settles, which current pages link. The
  answer is quoted where the owner's words were recorded, and marked as a paraphrase
  where they were not. Where the owner chose an option the lead offered, the row records
  the owner's answer and not the whole text of the option. The same change marks the
  design note or research page the decision settles `historical`.
- **A later decision that replaces an earlier one** moves the earlier row to the
  archive's [replaced](owner-decisions-archive.md#replaced) section, with what replaced
  it. A later decision that only narrows an earlier one leaves the earlier row
  here, linked to the later one. Rows are not deleted.
- **A one-off approval or a ruling on work that is finished** (a run, a spend, a
  removal, a study's design) moves to the archive's
  [used approvals and rulings](owner-decisions-archive.md#used-approvals-and-rulings-on-finished-work).
  It grants nothing further.
- **Evidence** links the record, ADR, page or issue that holds the detail.
- **Names.** A row names no coding agent, provider or model; where the owner's quoted
  words name one, a bracketed description stands in for the name
  ([decision](#no-agent-provider-or-model-named-outside-operations)).

Rows dated before 13 November 2026 were collected on that day from the dated records,
the ADRs, the current pages and the open issues. Not included: decisions recorded only
in comments on closed issues, and nine entries whose author could not be confirmed as
the owner.
