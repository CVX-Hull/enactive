# Project management

The user owns product direction and constraints. The lead owns design, briefs,
integration, escalation and final verification under [S-AGENT](standards/agents.md#s-agent-accountable-agent-assisted-work).
These pages define how work becomes a reviewable outcome. [Engineering](engineering.md)
owns mandatory standards; [research](research-engineering.md#project-management-basis--2026-10-17)
records external support and limits.

| [Development-first delivery](workflows/development.md) | Checkpoints from development play to formal acceptance and merge, gameplay as the feedback loop, minimum checks and escalation |
| [Review by decision and risk](workflows/review.md) | Which review a change needs, risk-based assurance and milestone review |
| [Protected CI profiles](workflows/ci-profiles.md) | What the required check runs on pull requests and on main |
