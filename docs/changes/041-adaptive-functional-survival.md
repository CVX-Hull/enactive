play. Allow bounded exploration while current visible needs, carried usable stock,
a credible next-decision margin and an assessed return/escape option support it.
Unknown rooms and terrain are frontiers to inspect, not presumed safe or empty.
Use existing native Look/Overmap/inventory/examine, guarded short walks or individually
assessed moves. A stopped walk still requires separate fresh Observe, interpretation
and deliberate Resume; a friendly creature is not permission for an automatic
fallback. Preserve relative-position invalidation on reload. Do not plan from hidden
saves/mapgen or promote remembered terrain to current visibility.

The first assignment must prioritize water/access and food coverage, not training.
Known remaining leads are an actually non-openable interior door (makeshift crowbar
lacks leverage), uninspected rooms and bounded exterior reconnaissance; none is a
verified source. The operator chooses an actual feasible route and reassesses cost
before a nested material/tool chain. Ordinary engine-validated access actions may
be chosen deliberately, including a finite assessed bash if justified; no forced
engine bypass or automatic repetition. Avoid hostile approaches; inspect threat,
retreat path and costs before any newly visible threat decision. No promise of
unimplemented combat/semantic-monitoring capability.

Do not bank long optional training, waiting or rest on an unverified supply chain.
Require actual potable replenishment/treatment/output and quantity/access coverage
for the chosen next interval. Preserve stock for return/needs; if Thirsty or hungry,
address it before optional work. No untreated-water drinking, starvation strategy,
unsafe fire, capped-skill padding or assumed sleep. If current conditions no longer
support another bounded decision or retreat, Pause and return to the lead.
