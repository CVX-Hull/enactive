## One coherent campaign

1. Freeze the final protected source/native/configuration, new world/character,
   player role, update policy, total/root/child limits and ending provisions.
   Verify no stale owned runtime, background/windowed options and actual structured
   tool access without routine approval review. Use ordinary default Evacuee/
   Survivor creation and random location; no guaranteed-supply map or save edits.
2. The sole player owns survival plans, visible needs/threat assessment, supplies,
   water treatment where needed, shelter/rest and purposeful travel. Semantic
   skills compose work; engine outcomes require visible verification. Record
   corrections and unfinished Work naturally, not by manufacturing a failure.
3. At each elapsed24-hour milestone, retain the actual turn/needs/supplies/shelter
   evidence, uncertainty and cumulative joint forecast. Record meaningful supply,
   travel and threat decisions across the run; merely advancing a clock in shelter
   is insufficient. The lead may inspect engineering evidence but supplies no
   segment tactics or day-by-day gameplay approval.
4. At a meaningful settled boundary, issue one real retained-memory handoff to a
   clean-context successor. Software retains required Work/source lineage; the
   successor retrieves actual images, claims once and separately readmits without
   a new clock/cost allowance. The lead's orchestration transfers original brief
   and registered references only. An advisory learner is not required.
5. Normally save/quit at a midpoint, verify owned child exit and launch a new child
   for exact same-character reload. Compare world/character and saved/loaded turn;
   never replay a loading confirmation with unknown delivery. Choose handoff and
   restart order from actual supported conditions rather than a tactical script.
6. At or beyond604800elapsed game seconds within the fixed cap, verify the same
   survivor's essential needs, retained evidence and original campaign outcomes.
   Normally save/quit and Finish, permanently quiesce the actor, then verify owned
   native/controller shutdown. Unsupported interfaces, genuine authority/storage
   faults or resource exhaustion may produce an honest failed/partial run.
