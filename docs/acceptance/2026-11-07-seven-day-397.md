`sd_admit.py` refuses to start an admission in each of these cases:
- a started admission lacks a final spend row;
- the admission is out of order;
- for admission N > 1, the lead's D4 review of admission N−1 is missing or does not decide to continue;
- E1 fails under `tools/run_ledger.py totals --study 397 --envelope 150` (admissions 1–5) or `--envelope 300` (from admission 6, F2);
- the decision allowance is spent;
- a game is already running;
- a pre-built admission 1 is no longer fresh (a store exists, or the userdir is not pristine).

The game lock belongs to the run worktree, so the start-time check is the only guard against a game launched from the main checkout. The lead launches no other game while an admission runs.

Final rows are recorded with `tools/run_ledger.py record`. When the lineage ends, they are copied into the tracked ledger, and this record is added to the ledger's cross-checked hand tables.

## Stop rules and classification (fixed before any run)

The lineage ends at the first of:
1. **168 elapsed in-game hours**, from admission 1's first in-play clock. This is checked at each admission boundary, so the last admission may run past it.
2. **Death.**
3. **5,000 decisions.** Started attempts bound decisions, so the allowance is `min(400, 5,000 − the lineage's started attempts)`, and an allowance of 20 or fewer ends the lineage.
4. **A continuity failure.**
5. **Slow:** two consecutive custody stops with one cause.

The lead stops and asks the owner in any of these cases:
- a continuity failure;
- a third custody stop;
- the envelope is reached;
- any change to the frozen package would be needed.

Each lineage gets exactly one class, checked in this order:
1. **Continuity failure:** an admission does not resume the same survivor in the same world, or an admission starts a new episode. Under #382's D7, which carries over, a setup restart is not a continuity failure: the player enters play for one decision with no game time, saves and quits, then creates the survivor the lineage carries. The evaluator does not flag this, so the lead rules on it from frames.
2. **Died on day N:** a death before 168 elapsed hours.
3. **Seven-day:** alive at 168 elapsed hours.
4. **Slow:** alive under 168 elapsed hours when the decision cap or a stop rule ends it.

**Day checks.** A day check falls at every 24 elapsed hours. Each uses #382's D3 rule and severe-need list: alive, with no severe thirst, hunger or rest. The rule is `lineage.day_check` applied at d × 24 h by `sd_days.py`. The lead confirms every day point, boundary and death by hand from frames. The record states how many of the seven checks passed. Under F1, a failed check does not end the lineage.

Between admissions the lead:
- scores the admission with `evaluate-run`;
- stitches the lineage with `evaluate-lineage`;
- runs `sd_days.py`;
- confirms the boundary and any day point from frames;
- reviews #382's D4 Pause conditions;
- writes the review record;
- posts a short report on #397 at each day point.

## Result

**Class: Died on day 2.** The death came at **47.79 elapsed hours**, 13 minutes before the day 2 check. The hand check from frames:
1. `sd1a7`'s second actor chose a new 4× clean water batch (frame `-11039`). At the prompt for "the in progress clean water (0%)" it chose "Put it down and start working" (`-11261`), and at 7:47:10 AM on day 63 (`-11479`) the log reads "You start working on the in progress clean water (0%)". The fire burned on the next tile with 20 splintered wood on it, and 6 more lay on the player's own tile.
2. The log shows "You're on fire!" and "You burn your legs and feet!".
3. It tried to put the fire out, but the fire spread. At 7:47:26 AM (`-12111`) the frame shows severe pain, every stat at 1, and "You're lying in fire!".
4. The memorial (`-12318`) reads "In memory of: Morgan Hale. Survived: 1d 23h 47m". The player's final report agrees and states that no debug or cheats were used.

This is a self-inflicted hazard while crafting, not needs, combat or shelter.

**Day checks:** 1 reached, 1 passed (day 1). Days 2–7 were not reached. Under F1 a failed check would not have ended the lineage, but none failed.
