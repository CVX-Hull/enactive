# CDDA recurrence preparation

## At a glance

[#668](https://github.com/CVX-Hull/enactive/issues/668) delivers a small, reproducible
preparation package under `tools/recurrence_research/`. It inventories retained
reusable sources, builds one real visible-source packet, demonstrates inert
schema/value edits and a selected readout, reduces one fixed inventory question,
and computes conservative proposed model bills. **No model, CDDA, source acquisition
or research comparison ran.** Three genuinely independent source slots remain
unfilled; the inspected traces cannot supply the proposed diversity by copying them.

The [proposal](../design/structured-recurrence-research.md#implemented-no-model-preparation)
owns how to reproduce this slice and what the later experiment would test.
[`spec.json`](../../data/recurrence-research/spec.json) owns its candidate, prompts,
role ceilings, profiles, grouping/scoring specification and explicit unpriced work.
The current player/curator publication interfaces were not changed.

## Stand-in cycle and outcome

The candidate starts with generic records (`id`, `body`, source IDs), without a game
ontology. The deterministic update adds an optional generic string field to its
schema and validates a scratch migrated state. A complete research publication
records its parent, coverage and hashes. A failed migration leaves it unchanged;
editing-disabled execution still applies valid generic value updates. Subsequent
value-only updates retain the published schema. Retrieval selects the inventory
record for a fixed question, followed by a source-citing, nondispatched ESC decision.
The case also records stale coverage, a contradicted interpretation and an unknown
future-travel answer. Reset restores the initial bytes.

These are hand-authored stand-ins. No structure emerged from a learner. The source
→ edit → research publication → selected readout → proposed decision join is explicit;
historical public intent/hints, actual delivery and subsequent action effect remain
unavailable. Arbitrary schemas cannot pass through the current fixed fact/article
gate and `delivered-knowledge/1`. This package maps those existing interfaces and
does not simulate a successful production publication.

The fixed inventory question is independent of candidate output. One assigned row
has visible positive support; the reduction reports assigned=1, positive=1. Unknown
and technical-failure/censoring sensitivity branches retain their denominator and
are labelled hypothetical, not additional trials. A contrary “no weapon” claim is
visibly contradicted; no negative gameplay outcome is manufactured. This is one
deterministic reduction, not a general scorer, calibration or effect estimate.

The pilot includes 90 acquisition/load/cleanup requests, 90 continuation requests
(two of ten per continuation reserved for ending), and curator, recall and judge
allocations. Failures/retries consume these original caps; exhaustion stops the
attempt. Replay includes one candidate proposal per arm, updates, selected readouts,
player decisions and two independent labels. This is smaller than the later proposed
12-candidate-per-arm search, which is excluded and unpriced here.

All role profiles propose pinned OpenAI Terra through OpenRouter, medium effort and
default tier. Player/recall retain the current configured capacities; curator/search
and judge caps are explicitly proposed separately. Decimal arithmetic computes each
bound from the most expensive configured input rate ($1.25/M) and output ($5/M),
without assuming cache savings. Current metadata was verified during this session;
these are configured-rate bounds, not measured expected bills or an immutable provider
tariff. A later admitted package must use its exact current route and price.

Native/human durations, possible infrastructure charges and acquisition feasibility
are unknown. Extra requests, replacements, larger searches and six-profile coverage
are outside these finite allocations. The worksheet therefore supplies known model
ceilings and explicit gaps, not a complete approval-ready pilot total. No earlier
balance or standing premerge allowance funds this research.

The next paid protocol must resolve feasible independent sources and supported
readout integration. [#681](https://github.com/CVX-Hull/enactive/issues/681) retains
visible-outcome reader qualification; later sizing must use the exact all-assigned
paired/profile-weighted estimator, world/lineage clustering, unknown/censoring and
complete search-repeat uncertainty. [#677](https://github.com/CVX-Hull/enactive/issues/677)
can reuse these packets. The twelve-source coverage proposal and old withholding
forecasts supply no sample-size claim. Actual acquisition, replay and play require
their separately priced admission and owner envelope.
