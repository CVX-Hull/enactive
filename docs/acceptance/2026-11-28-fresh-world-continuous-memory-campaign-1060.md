## Why multi-actor handoff and interleaved hygiene did not execute

1. **Interleaved hygiene dual gate mismatch (`live_runner.py:354-369` & `runner.py:818-847`):**
   - **Primary blocker — `10%` spend share vs. `min_headroom`:** `HygieneSessions.min_headroom()` computes `_min_session_headroom(prices, "terra", max_output_tokens=16384) = $0.0832`. `HygieneSessions.pass_cap()` caps a hygiene pass at `min($0.30, total_spent * 0.10, total_remaining)`. For `available()` (`pass_cap >= min_headroom`) to ever return `True`, **within-admission live curator spend must reach `$0.832`** (`10 × $0.0832`). Because `40/60` `openai/flex` curation cost only **`$0.0563`/window**, live curator spend peaked at `$0.2056` (A01), `$0.5246` (A02), and `$0.5643` (A03) — never once reaching `$0.832`, even though `hygiene_trigger(repo.records())` returned `triggered: True` continuously from `v13` through `v37` (`stale_citations` and `total_advisory >= 8`).
   - **Secondary blocker — `max_unexamined_steps: 10` vs. `flex` window duration:** At `50–134 s` per `flex` window and `~6.4 s`/decision player pace, the player typically advanced `11–20` steps while a window ran.
