## Smallest useful new capture

Prepare one fresh CDDA development world with a limited serial continuation
under the current player. Its purpose is to acquire a verified, later decision after the
player has explored at least one different useful activity. The first package
can allow one initial admission and at most one eligible same-survivor
continuation. Freeze wall, decision, capture and provider-charge limits,
checkpoints and the ending reserve before launch. Keep any separate campaign
acceptance with [#145](https://github.com/CVX-Hull/enactive/issues/145).

- Record a useful world-specific fact when the player naturally encounters it.
  Prefer a location, resource, changed condition or unfinished task that
  plausibly matters again. Keep the earlier named survivor, actual pixels,
  clock, original operation and objective.
- Seek a return after a natural later waking period and a real context
  boundary, with **8–24 in-game hours** as a proposed sizing range. This is
  a design choice to cover a later waking period, based on useful #755 replay
  cases near 15 and 22 hours. Shorter useful cases remain separate. Continued
  play is useful if it creates actual choices. Sleep and waiting alone do
  not fulfill the capture. Keep ordinary handoff and recent context intact,
  and retain the exact manifests, inspectable successor inputs and memory
  deliveries. Use a fresh actor for the continuation. The manifest identifies
  parts by digest. Preserve unknown exposure if the retained material cannot
  establish the relevant ordinary content.
- Before the later choice, retain the current view, feasible alternatives,
  and the exact common context. After the choice, retain the visible result,
  time, name and living state. Treat a fact still in common context as a
  control. Seek justified verification where the older fact may have become
  stale. Freeze candidate inputs at the decision and keep later frames with
  the evaluator.
- Close the one-world pilot after one independently scoreable opportunity with
  the earlier fact absent from ordinary context and a verified ending, or at
  the allotted stop. Preserve every control, miss, death and unknown. Follow-up in a second independently created world is
  worthwhile when the first proves the evidence can be captured. A causal
  comparison still needs matched arms and genuinely independent data.
