correctness or arm identity. An inert decoder and gapless interval checks establish
the recorded text; finite supplements verify the exact result of its corresponding
own-batch read or numeric slice. Private reports and diagnostic failures remain.

Two formatter cases needed supplements rather than model redos. Reader 033 had
recovered its middle interval with a two-argument slice. Reader 158 had recovered

The automatic reports remain unchanged. The final 12 first refusals have no
complete presentation, and Reader 088's repeated 227-character omission was
independently confirmed. No scientific response was used to choose refusals.

proof is supplemented by the complete absolute-own-batch-literal scope check;

was mislabeled through a loop-variable collision; the corrected version reproduced
all proof fields and proper batch identities. The defective receipt is retained
and was not adopted. Existing development admission decisions stayed unchanged.

Recorded presentation establishes neither attention, provider receipt nor OS
