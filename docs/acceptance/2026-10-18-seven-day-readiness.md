independent review found no confirmed P1/P2 defect. The lead accepts its design
for protected publication. **No fresh character or seven-day campaign has been

The user-facing plan is one fresh ordinary character, seven bounded daily
assignments and a real player-context replacement at the midpoint. Each day is
300 charged inputs / two wall hours; the whole attempt is 2600 / 24 hours,
including creation, recovery, review and publication. A failed, dead, partial or
unknown attempt remains such. There is no automatic second character or renewed
allowance. The detailed protocol owns the activity, game-time and storage limits.
