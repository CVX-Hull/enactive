# Retained two-wake decision audit (#777)

## At a glance

Three new cases from two inspected development worlds yielded one
named visible effect with ordinary cues, one ordinary source return
with a confirmed pickup and unknown near-effect identity, and one
vehicle attempt with an unconfirmed unlock. A 64k input target
changed which details survived. None of these cases joined an
actionable ordinary-access gap to a named visible effect. The next
proposed package is one longer, prospective acquisition centered on
returning to an earlier tool or unfinished job. This review used
retained observations and local packing with $0 provider spend.

## Disposition

Prepare one fresh sheltered capability contrast with a credible
ordinary equipment-return or unfinished-work opportunity. Select the
initial kit and site from rendered options. Keep the player in charge
of its actual objective and route. Budget for two natural wakes and
about 48–60 game hours in one lineage. Use more of the same world,
up to the existing 72-hour design boundary, when a prospectively
recorded later choice remains feasible. Freeze the finite slot,
capture and provider-cost bounds before pricing a launch.

Enroll the first applicable before-action choice under a frozen
rule. Seek a fact that becomes useful again after roughly 24–48
game hours. Capture the earlier tool or substitute, its source and age,
what makes it usable, the later alternatives and exact ordinary
inputs. Record the effort to look it up or check it in the world.
Capture the executed visible effect and a named living view within
five visible operations in the same task episode. Keep failed and
unknown assignments. Use #774's neutral
request notice and #775's independently verified reload rule on
the selected source. Future paid work requires its own approved
envelope. [Acquisition design](../design/delayed-memory-assessment.md#what-to-capture-and-when)
