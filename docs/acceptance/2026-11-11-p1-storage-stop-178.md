## Engineering disposition

The [#540 follow-up](https://github.com/CVX-Hull/enactive/issues/540#issuecomment-6806406822)
retains the conservative storage guard and adds initial/minimum/last/floor readings,
typed initial/poll read failures, no-launch headroom refusal, and profile cleanup
protection. Measurements propagate through session and shadow receipts. This improves
diagnosis and safe admission; it does not eliminate shared-volume false stops.

A local disconnect test first failed: a client reset before response headers caused
available upstream usage to be replaced by a reservation and revocation. The repair
continues draining and settling after header-delivery failure. Only idle keep-alive
resets are quiet; active accounting failures remain visible and latched. Payloads,
routes, retries and caps are unchanged. Future replay launches drain the maintained
runner's durable output without the redundant observer interrupt. Intentional user
interruption, hard termination and unwritable storage retain their limits.

Local focused verification passed 231 tests, including the real macOS Seatbelt lane,
scripted loopback proxy/accounting, interruption/cleanup and replay-stop tests; two
dependency deprecation warnings remain. After a final observation-order correction,
the affected storage tests were rechecked. Fast structural checks passed. Exact protected
PR/main evidence and independent finding dispositions belong to #540; source tests and
native fixtures do not establish provider reliability or a successful study.
